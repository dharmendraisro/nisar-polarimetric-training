# Training Workflow

## Foundation: Modules 01–12

01. Setup and product selection
02. Session check and product orientation
03. L1 metadata-first exploration
04. L2 GCOV exploration
05. Large-data performance and windowed access
06. AOI and spatial subsetting
07. QC, statistics and masks
08. Visualization and RGB/GCS
09. Advanced frequency/mask/metadata/batch processing
10. GeoTIFF export and QGIS validation
11. End-to-end GCOV capstone
12. Trainer troubleshooting and session management

## Polarimetric extension: Modules 13–19

13. Polarimetric mode discovery and covariance inventory
14. LSAR full-pol covariance reconstruction
15. Full-pol observables and eigen analysis
16. Cloude–Pottier H/A/alpha
17. Pauli / Freeman–Durden / Yamaguchi
18. SSAR compact-pol Stokes and m-chi/m-delta/m-alpha
19. End-to-end polarimetric capstone

The polarimetric modules use the persisted spatial subset established by Module 06. They do not introduce a second participant-facing AOI workflow.
